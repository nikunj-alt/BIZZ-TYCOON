Trouble, detected.

GASTECT is a small, always-on sensor hub for gas and fire. It reads the room every second, flags danger in three clear levels, and shows you exactly what it sees — no cloud, no monthly fee, no noise.

Most alarms tell you after.
GASTECT tells you before.

Gas leaks don't announce themselves. A small, continuous, watching system can be the difference between a nuisance and an emergency.

01

Ten sensors, one board

MQ-2 gas, flame, PIR motion, light, touch, joystick, buzzer, servo valve, battery sense — all on an Arduino Uno that costs less than a pizza.

02

A full pipeline under your roof

Arduino → Bluetooth → a Python WebSocket bridge → a live dashboard. No cloud. Your data never leaves your network, and the whole thing runs on a laptop.

03

Three clear states

SAFE, WARNING, DANGER — each with its own light and tone, so you know exactly what to do without decoding a screen.

04

You can inspect every layer

The firmware, the bridge, and the dashboard are open source. If you want to change a threshold or add a sensor, the whole path is readable end to end.
